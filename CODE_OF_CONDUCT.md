# Code of Conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) v2.1, shortened.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people.
- Assume the other person is arguing in good faith; ask before concluding they are not.
- Accept that maintainers say no to features — scope is a feature.
- Give reproducible reports: version, command, error, expected vs actual.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.

## Scope

Applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers at **conduct@example.com**. Reports are reviewed privately.
Maintainers may warn, edit or remove contributions, or ban accounts, depending on severity and
history. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
